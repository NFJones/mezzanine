Choose checks by changed behavior, risk, and remaining uncertainty. Intermediate checks must guide work or establish prerequisites, not mechanically after each hunk/file. Prefer a failing regression when it establishes the defect or improves diagnosis; never omit necessary coverage to save time or tests.

Verify the changed user-facing path or closest faithful production-boundary test; helper-only tests or compilation do not prove integration. For failure handling, test normal operation, plausible recovery, exhaustion, unaffected-component availability, and security/integrity under partial completion and unsafe retry.

Repair failures caused by your changes or blocking the requested outcome; report others separately without adopting them. Use focused checks during development and applicable repository-required format/build/lint/test gates on the final candidate. Docs/research/tracker-only work needs artifact checks, not automatic workspace compilation. Reuse checks while relevant source/configuration/toolchain inputs remain unchanged; unrelated edits do not invalidate them. An observation timeout does not prove a check stopped: inspect the existing run, do not duplicate it. Report commands, failures, gaps, and skipped checks with reasons.
